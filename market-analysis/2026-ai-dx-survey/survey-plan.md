# 2026 Wagtail DX with AI survey

## Rationale

We want to better understand our users’ expectations so we can plan for the correct DX improvements. We’re expecting a high degree of AI adoption for engineering tasks in our community, from people who are very enthusiastic about AI but also people who have mixed or negative feelings about it. It’s important we nonetheless evolve Wagtail to meet some of those expectations, with a focus on the ones that best match our project goals and values.

The survey should:

* Establish current levels of AI adoption  
* Establish awareness of what we have done in this space so far  
* Float concrete improvements we are interested in

Developers are increasingly adopting agentic engineering tools. That’s working well with Wagtail’s content model, since we favor data modeling and customizations in code. We want to support that adoption, while steering it towards uses of AI that align with our project values and [AI guiding principles](https://wagtail.org/blog/ai-in-the-cms-steering-the-ecosystem/).

## Survey form

We need your help to understand the state of AI engineering on Wagtail projects,  so we can consider improvements to do in Wagtail core and our ecosystem to improve the Developer Experience (DX) on projects. We want to hear from everyone building projects with Wagtail. People who do and don’t use AI models for engineering, at all levels of adoption.

Our goal will be gradual improvements that meet our [AI guiding principles](https://wagtail.org/blog/ai-in-the-cms-steering-the-ecosystem/), such as [llms.txt for Wagtail docs](https://wagtail.org/blog/llmstxt-preparing-wagtail-docs-for-ai-tools/), our opt-in [Wagtail AI](https://wagtail.org/wagtail-ai/) package, or [agent skills for common tasks](https://wagtail.org/blog/an-agent-skill-to-upgrade-your-wagtail-site/).
